# Understanding Our Unit Staking System

Our tips show stakes in **units** so readers can compare the relative size of selections without assuming that everyone has the same bankroll or uses the same currency. A unit is a measuring convention, not a fixed cash amount set by The Expertise Wins.

## What is a unit?

Choose a cash value for one unit that is small and suitable for your own circumstances. Keep that value consistent when reviewing a run of tips. For example, if you decide that one unit represents KSh 100, a two-unit stake would represent KSh 200 for you. Someone else may choose a different value—or may follow the tips for analysis without placing a bet.

The example is only arithmetic. It is not a recommended stake or a promise about returns. Do not choose a unit using money needed for rent, food, bills, debt payments, or other essentials.

## How to read the stake on a tip

The unit figure describes the stated stake for that selection. A four-unit selection has a larger stated stake than a one-unit selection; it does not mean the selection is four times more likely to win. Odds and analysis provide different information, and every sporting outcome remains uncertain.

Some tips include more than one selection for an event. Read the stake beside each selection separately. If you choose to follow several selections, consider the total amount at risk across them; related selections can lose together. Do not treat multiple picks as a guaranteed combination or add their stakes without considering that combined exposure.

## Our published limit is seven units

**Seven units is the highest stake we will publish on a selection.** Most of our stakes are below that cap. A higher unit figure is not a claim of certainty, and the cap is not a target that every tip should reach. Stake sizes can vary with the selection and its context; they do not remove risk or guarantee a result.

We will not ask readers to increase their unit value to recover a loss. Keep your own unit size fixed, set a limit before betting, and stop when you reach it. Never chase losses or borrow to bet.

## Reviewing the record

For a meaningful review, consider stake alongside odds, outcome, and the time period. A win rate alone does not show how much was staked or returned. Pending selections are not settled results, and short runs can be heavily affected by chance. Our archive and statistics are records of past selections, not forecasts of future performance.

The Expertise Wins provides sports analysis for transparency. It does not provide guaranteed income or remove the risks of betting. Only bet where legal and only if you meet the local age requirement. If betting stops feeling manageable, take a break and seek support.
