# The Golden Rules of Bankroll Management for Long-Term Profitability

Even with a verified **74%+ win rate**, improper staking can wipe out a betting account during unavoidable variance streaks. Mastering bankroll management is the single most important skill for long-term betting success.

---

## 1. The 1% - 3% Unit Rule

Never stake arbitrary amounts based on emotion or excitement. Set a fixed unit size representing **1% to 3%** of your total starting bankroll.

- **Starting Bankroll**: $1,000
- **1 Unit (1%)**: $10
- **Max Stake (3%)**: $30

## 2. Flat Staking vs. Proportional Staking

### Flat Staking
Wager the exact same dollar amount ($10 per tip) regardless of confidence level. This eliminates emotional decision-making and protects your balance.

### Proportional Staking
Adjust stake size based on verified value. Higher-confidence selections (e.g. MaxBet VIP tips) receive 2 units, while standard free tips receive 1 unit.

---

## 3. Avoid The Chasing Trap

The fastest way to bust a bankroll is "chasing losses" by doubling stakes after a lost match. Trust the system, stick to your predefined unit size, and let the law of large numbers work in your favor.
